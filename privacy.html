Privacy Policy for Helix Stack Crash: Smash Ball

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Helix Stack Crash: Smash Ball (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal information ourselves. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own.

Game progress (levels reached, high score, coins, unlocked skins, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Third-party services

The App uses the following Google services, which may collect and process information under their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob (advertising)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics/crash data

This data is used to deliver, measure, and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent through Google’s consent message before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads).

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Optional in-app purchases (coin packs, shield boost pack, premium skins, remove ads) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or billing details. Google Play gives the App only a purchase confirmation (product and purchase token) so the item can be delivered and restored.

Google Play terms: https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through a third-party service in the App, contact us and we will help.

Data security and retention

We do not hold your data. Data processed by Google is retained and secured according to Google’s policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to processing of your data. Because we do not store personal data, please use the controls above or contact Google for data held by AdMob or Google Play. You may also contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this page with a new “Last updated” date.
